# 2.3 — Send a store only the families of its stock point (analysis, not built)

Written 2026-10-10 during 2.2.2 (read only). Decision (Zein): not in 2.2.2, planned for 2.3. Today
`HoCatalogueService` (head office whose catalogue comes from the ERP) sends every family and sub-family to every store;
items and barcodes are already filtered by the rows of the store's stock point (PS).

## Places to change (head office)

1. `load()` for FAMILY and SUBFAMILY: answer only the codes used by the store's PS rows (`row.familyCode`,
   `row.subFamilyCode`, the codes the item copy already uses, not the item's own family) plus the families of the items
   made by hand (not from the ERP, they go to every store). A code not answered is "removed" at the store.
2. `stockPointRowsChanged`: also record the family and sub-family codes for the stores of that PS. Without it a family
   newly used by a PS is never sent again and stays inactive at the store.
3. `storeStockPointChanged`: also record every family and sub-family code (a few hundred).
4. A one-time resend (start-up step guarded by a General Setup flag): every family and sub-family code for every store,
   so existing stores switch the unused ones off.
5. Families of hand-made items: unchanged recording (every store), and `load()` must keep them.
- Unchanged: the save hooks, the ERP family/sub-family sync (`HeadOfficeErpCatalogueRecorder`) and the start-up
  `backfill`/`currentTargets` record for every store; `load()` decides what each store gets.

## Store side

`CatalogueCopyWriter`: a missing family makes the item WAITING (retried every cycle); a removed code becomes inactive
(head office origin only); a family sent again is set active again. Note: an item finds its family by code even when the
family is inactive, so an item can be applied under a family still inactive until the family arrives.

## Risks

- Promotions by family (`PromotionDownHandler` resolves the family by code): a promotion for a family a store never had
  stays WAITING for ever at that store (nothing to sell there; noise on the link page).
- A store with the purchase right whose own items use a head office family not in its PS: the family is switched off, its
  own items disappear from the POS grid. Needs a store-side guard (do not switch off a family used by the store's own items).
- A family switched off then used again: comes back only if point 2 records families.
- Order of records: within a page families come first (fine); across pages a short WAITING.
- Reports at the store: family filters may hide the families now inactive.

## Cost (ZEN: 21 PS, ~50 000 items each; estimate, not measured)

`load()` for a family page: one `select distinct family_code, sub_family_code from ho_stock_point_item where
stock_point_id = ?` over ~50 000 rows. The unique index (stock_point_id, item_id) gives the range but the family columns
need lookups: tens to a couple of hundred ms; cache per PS, refreshed when its rows change, or add an index
(stock_point_id, family_code, sub_family_code) (a database change). Recording: a few hundred codes per PS change; the
one-time resend about (families + sub-families) x stores rows (~1 000 x 50 = 50 000 rows, once).

## Delivery

One separate commit (head office service, one-time flag, store guard, tests), easy to leave out of a build. Tests: two
PS with different families (each store gets its own only); a hand-made item's family reaches both; a row change adding a
family records it for the PS's stores; a store's PS change resends families; the one-time resend switches unused
families off at the store and back on when used again (store round trip); the store guard keeps a family used by its own
items; a family promotion at a store outside the family waits (documented). Size and risk: medium (small head office
change; the risk is in the store-side effects and the one-time switch-off on stores that have everything).
